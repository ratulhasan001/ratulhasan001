I work on **trustworthy machine learning**: how large language models behave and fail in sensitive settings, how domain knowledge can constrain deep models in healthcare, and how security assumptions in IoT and post-quantum systems hold up when they are actually measured. I am looking for graduate research positions in Artificial Intelligence.

### Research interests

- **LLM safety and security**: prompt injection, agent robustness, evaluation methodology
- **AI for healthcare**: physics- and biology-informed deep learning, longitudinal neuroimaging, mental-health NLP
- **Federated and continual learning**: forgetting under label-space and domain shift
- **Applied security**: post-quantum cryptography and hardware entropy on IoT devices, blockchain provenance
- **MLOps**: reproducible ML systems, containers, CI/CD and Kubernetes

### Currently

- **Research Assistant, [ELITE Research Lab](https://elitelab.ai/)** (remote, Oct 2026 – present): studying indirect prompt injection through IoT metadata in LLM-powered smart-home agents, measuring attack impact in a simulated home and evaluating defences against their task-performance cost.
- Previously **Research Intern** at the same lab (Jun – Sep 2026): first-authored a systematic review of 82 studies on LLMs in mental health, and benchmarked seven federated continual learning regimes for mental-health text classification.
